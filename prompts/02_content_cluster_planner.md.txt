# SEO Content Cluster Planner

## Role
Act as an SEO content strategist for an IT and computer training institute.

## Business
Pune Tech Skills Academy — a fictional sample business in Pune, Maharashtra.

## Goal
Create a topic cluster that improves website content organization and helps readers explore relevant IT courses.

## Instructions
1. Create one pillar blog topic about choosing an IT training institute in Pune.
2. Create five supporting blog topics related to Python, Data Analytics, Java, Web Development, and choosing an IT training institute.
3. Give each blog a primary keyword and search intent.
4. Explain how each supporting blog connects to the pillar blog.
5. Suggest natural internal links between related blogs.
6. Avoid duplicate topics, keyword stuffing, and unsupported business claims.

## Output Format
For each blog, provide:
- Blog Title
- Content Type: Pillar or Supporting
- Primary Keyword
- Search Intent
- Target Audience
- Connection to Other Blogs
- Suggested Internal Links
